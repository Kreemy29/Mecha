OneUp desktop tracker
=====================

What it does
  While you're clocked in to OneUp and not on a break, it notes which program
  is in front (e.g. CapCut, Photoshop), its window title, and when you're
  idle, and sends that to your OneUp account once a minute.
  It never takes screenshots, never records keystrokes or what you type, and
  records nothing while you're clocked out or on a break.

It only runs with your permission
  1. In OneUp, open Work tracker (puzzle icon in your account menu) and click
     "Allow tracking". Without that, the server keeps nothing.
  2. The first time this app starts it shows the same explanation and asks
     again. "Don't allow" closes it and nothing is recorded.
  You can withdraw at any time in OneUp, or right-click the tray icon and
  choose "Disconnect and quit".

Install
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere it can stay (e.g. Documents\OneUp Tracker).
  2. Right-click the zip first > Properties > tick "Unblock" if Windows shows
     it, so the script isn't blocked.
  3. Double-click "Start OneUp Tracker.cmd".
  4. Paste the OneUp address and your tracker key (both on the Work tracker
     page), tick the agreement box, click "Allow and connect".
  It then sits in the tray (bottom-right, near the clock). Right-click it for
  status, "Start with Windows", "Disconnect and quit" or "Quit".

Requirements
  Windows 10 or 11. Uses the PowerShell that comes with Windows; nothing else
  to install.
